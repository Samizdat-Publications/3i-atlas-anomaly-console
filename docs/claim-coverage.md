# Claim coverage — the 24-anomaly list against the console's register

Source: the 2026-03-16 episode "3I Atlas — The Final Chapter! Here's 24 reasons I
think 3I Atlas is an Alien Asteroid Ship!", read in full from the transcript
archive. He presents 22 anomalies drawn from Avi Loeb's published list, then adds
2 of his own.

Purpose: find out what the console's 26 3I/ATLAS case files already answer and
what they miss. Compiled 2026-08-23; re-checked 2026-08-24.

**Headline: coverage is complete, and the last two "partials" are closed as of
2026-08-24.** All 22 of his observational anomalies map onto case files. The ALMA
methanol gap was closed by A-26 on 2026-08-23. On re-check, the two remaining
partial rows turned out to be different problems: row 12 was a real gap INSIDE a
case — the Ni/CN ratio was stated in A-07's observation and argued in neither
direction — and has been argued in both; row 17 was never a gap at all, and this
document was simply out of date. His 2 additions are interpretations rather than
observations and need different handling.

No new case files were created for either. Splitting one set of measurements into
extra cases is exactly the inflation BR-01 warns about — a tally overstates the
evidence when one cause produces several of its entries — and the Ni/Fe and Ni/CN
values come from the same spectra.

## His two additions

These are not observations. They are **mechanisms proposed to explain the
observations above**, and treating them as further anomalies double-counts the
evidence — the same measurement appears once as a fact and again as its own
explanation.

**23. Deuterium as fusion fuel.** The elevated D/H ratio is read as a deuterium-rich
fuel supply for a compact fusion reactor, with the post-perihelion water spike as
vented coolant or reaction mass.

**24. Magnetohydrodynamic propulsion.** Methanol as ionisable propellant, seeded
with the detected nickel alloys, accelerated by an MHD drive — offered as a single
mechanism accounting for the collimated jets, the 120° mini-jets and the sideways
acceleration together. He credits a Medium article, not his own derivation.

Handling: the underlying measurements (D/H, methanol, Ni, jet geometry, sideways
NGA) are already case files. The proposed mechanism belongs in a claim block
attached to those cases, stated as the hypothesis it is, not as a 23rd and 24th
independent anomaly. Note that he presents it that way himself — "anomalies that
in my opinion scream techno signature much more than the previous 22" — so this is
a framing distinction, not a factual dispute.

## What to build

1. ~~**A-26 — methanol abundance (ALMA).**~~ **DONE 2026-08-23.** Verified against
   Roth, Cordiner et al., ApJL doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ae433b (arXiv:2511.20845).
   His account of the study is accurate, including the ratios and the C/2016 R2
   comparison. The decisive counterpoint is the paper's own: the single object
   that beats 3I/ATLAS on this measure formed in our solar system, so the ratio
   cannot mark an object as foreign by itself.
2. **Split the Ni/CN ratio out of A-07**, or state it explicitly within that case.
   It is a separate measurement against a separate comparison population.
3. **Add the ~20× post-perihelion water spike to A-20**, which currently covers only
   the disappearance of the icy-grain signature.
4. **A double-counting note.** When a mechanism is offered that explains several
   anomalies at once, the count of anomalies stops being the right measure of the
   evidence. That cuts both ways and is worth saying plainly: it weakens "24
   separate anomalies" as a tally, and it is also exactly what a correct unifying
   explanation would look like. The console should make that visible rather than
   pick a side.
