THE PREMISE

Three visitors. One of them is still being argued about.

In 2017 a tumbling, tail-less object called 1I/ʻOumuamua passed through and accelerated on its way out with nothing visibly pushing it. In 2019 2I/Borisov arrived and behaved like a perfectly ordinary comet. In 2025 3I/ATLAS came through on the most extreme hyperbolic orbit ever measured, and Harvard astronomer Avi Loeb began publishing a numbered list of things about it he considered anomalous — eventually reaching 22 items.

Most astronomers say it is a comet. Loeb says the list deserves an answer. This console does not pick for you: every case file states what was measured, what Loeb concluded, and what the mainstream explanation is — with sources — and then lets you fly the geometry yourself and see whether the story holds up.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

The aesthetic is fiction. The numbers are not.
TRAJECTORIES Daily state vectors for each object and all eight planets, pulled from the NASA/JPL Horizons system in heliocentric ecliptic J2000. Close approaches are computed from those vectors and match published values — 3I/ATLAS passes Mars at 0.194 AU on 2025-10-03 and reaches perihelion at 1.357 AU on 2025-10-29.
FIREBALLS The live NASA/JPL CNEOS Fireball Data API table — every atmospheric impact event US Government sensors have reported since 1988, with energy and, where published, position, altitude and speed. The two candidate rows are tagged by date rather than transcribed, so a catalog revision propagates. CNEOS publishes no uncertainties on any field, which is exactly what the IM1/IM2 dispute turns on. Coastlines are Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain.
CASE FILES Assembled by a multi-agent research pass, then put through per-case adversarial fact-checking against primary sources: arXiv, Nature, ApJL, ESO/ESA releases and Loeb's own essays. Each case displays its verdict CONFIRMED or CORRECTED and links to its references.
ILLUSTRATIONS Charts that depict a published result rather than plotting raw data — spectra, polarization curves, acceleration residuals — are stylized reconstructions and are labeled as such. Orbital geometry and the light-curve model are computed from the real ephemeris.
